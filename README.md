# SHNACKS – AI-Powered Sales & Operations Dashboard

An interactive web application built with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**, integrated with the **Google Gemini API** to demonstrate how AI and automation can streamline business operations, sales outreach, and customer qualification in real time.

---

## Key Features

* ** Performance Tracking & Analytics:** Real-time summary of business metrics (Total Revenue, Average Ticket, Units Sold, and Top Channels) paired with dynamic charts and one-click CSV export functionality.
* ** AI Sales Outreach Assistant:** Leverages Google Gemini AI to analyze every new order upon registration, suggesting personalized upselling strategies and customer follow-up actions.
* ** Customer & Lead Qualification:** Automatically collects and categorizes customer profiles, preferences, and sales channels to help prioritize high-value leads.
* ** Operational Automation:** Streamlines manual data entry through automated total calculations, timestamping, order status assignments, and local browser storage (`localStorage`) for offline availability.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **Icons:** Lucide React
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (with local analytical fallback)
* **Storage & Export:** LocalStorage API, CSV Generator
